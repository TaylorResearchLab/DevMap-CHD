To the extent legally possible, the rights holders in the DevMap-CHD-derived
data, provenance tables, release metadata, schemas, and repository-produced
documentation dedicate those materials to the public domain under
**CC0 1.0 Universal**.

Canonical CC0 deed and legal code:

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This dedication applies only to rights that the DevMap-CHD contributors are
authorized to waive or license. It does not alter rights in upstream
publications, source databases, trademarks, patents, or other third-party
materials.

DevMap-CHD does not redistribute raw upstream source files by default.
Instead, it distributes derived gene-membership and provenance records with
citations and exact source locators.

Although CC0 does not impose an attribution requirement, scientific users are
asked to cite the DevMap-CHD release and the originating sources represented in
`sources/source_manifest.tsv`.
